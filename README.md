# Retail Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer churn behavior using Power BI and SQL. The objective is to identify high-risk customers, understand churn drivers, and provide actionable business insights to improve customer retention.

## Business Problem
Customer churn directly impacts revenue and profitability. This dashboard helps businesses:

- Identify customers at risk of churning
- Analyze customer health and engagement
- Monitor loyalty and retention metrics
- Understand revenue contribution across customer segments

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- SQL
- Excel
- Data Visualization
- Data Cleaning

## Dataset
- 100,000 customer records
- Customer demographics
- Loyalty scores
- Engagement scores
- Purchase behavior
- Cart abandonment data
- Revenue and spending metrics

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
- Total Customers
- Total Revenue
- Average Order Value
- Average Loyalty Score
- At-Risk Customer Percentage
- Revenue by Region
- Revenue by Customer Segment
- Revenue by Age Group
- Revenue by Preferred Channel

### 2. Churn Risk Analysis
- At-Risk vs Healthy Customers
- Customer Health by Age Group
- Loyalty Score Analysis
- Engagement Score Analysis
- Customer Health Distribution
- Churn Insights and Recommendations

## Key Insights
- 89.4% of customers are classified as At Risk.
- East region has the highest number of at-risk customers.
- Customers with low loyalty scores show higher churn probability.
- High cart abandonment is associated with increased churn risk.
- Customer engagement plays a significant role in retention.

## Project Outcome
Developed an interactive dashboard that enables stakeholders to monitor churn trends, identify retention opportunities, and support data-driven decision-making.
